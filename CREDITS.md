# Crow Project Credits and Development Provenance

## Raven foundation

Crow is derived from and continues the
[Raven Mesh Messaging Platform](https://github.com/kn6plv/Raven). Raven
provided the original mesh-messaging codebase and Git history on which Crow
was built.

Credit for that foundation belongs to Raven's original developer,
Tim Wilkinson, and to every Raven contributor whose work remains represented
in this repository's commit history.

## Crow

Crow was conceived, invented, and directed by Mathison. Mathison defined the
project requirements, architecture, hardware integration, testing criteria,
acceptance decisions, and releases that distinguish Crow from its Raven
foundation.

The Crow implementation and documentation were developed through
human-directed, AI-assisted engineering. AI assistance included Bob, an
OpenAI Codex-based build assistant using OpenAI GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.6, with
additional assistance from Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude 4.6.

AI systems were used as engineering tools. They are not copyright holders or
legal authors. The human and project credits above do not replace the
individual attribution preserved in the Git history.
